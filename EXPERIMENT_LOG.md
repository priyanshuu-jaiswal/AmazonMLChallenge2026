## Stage 3.2 — additional normalization representations (2026-09-25)

An additional address representation canonicalized only observed sample variants `ST/Street`, `DR/Drive`, `AVE/Avenue`, and `LN/Lane` to full-word forms; it did not overwrite the conservative form. A further address representation converted conservative-normalized addresses to token sets; Jaccard was measured for inspection only. No data outside the supplied files was used. No full-dataset normalized copy was created.
- **Name/address exactness on 120 known matches:** Name exact equality 5 raw → 17 conservative-normalized.
